COMMON QUESTIONS

Good projects start
with clear answers.

How does the estimate process work?

We start with your project details, then schedule an on-site visit when needed so the proposal reflects the actual property and scope.

What types of projects do you handle?

Wood privacy, ornamental metal and gate options are featured in this demo. A live site can be tailored to the materials and styles actually offered.

How long does a typical project take?

Project timing depends on footage, materials, site conditions and permitting. The contractor can confirm the expected schedule after reviewing the property.

Do you handle fence repairs and replacements?

Repair availability varies by contractor and project. Ask about the existing fence when requesting an estimate.
